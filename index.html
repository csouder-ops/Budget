    Data

    Import takes any CSV with date, description and amount columns (or separate debit/credit columns). Export is a good backup since everything lives in this browser.

    Your data stays on your device. Everything is saved in this browser only. Nothing gets uploaded, there's no account, and nobody else who opens this page can see your numbers. Clearing your browser data will clear it, so export a backup now and then.
